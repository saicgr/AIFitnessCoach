Same blocks, same order. The prose becomes data, the paragraph becomes a greeting, and one band near the top shows a state instead of a queue.

The four changes

  1. To-do subtitles become fragments. "0.0L logged so far — top up to…" → "0.0 / 2.5 L". The truncation isn't just ugly, it's the worst case: it spends the space and withholds the answer. Same class as register #148.
  2. The coach copy stays — it becomes tappable. One truncated line plus a ›, so the full text is one tap away instead of always occupying three lines. The words were never the problem; permanently spending the screen's best real estate on them was.
  3. A visual band under the date — a chart, not just numerals. The single biggest reason the screen feels unimpressive is that nothing on it reflects an achievement; it's four outstanding obligations, which on day one reads as a nag. A 7-bar week (today highlighted) plus a streak ring gives the screen a shape you can read at a glance. All of it is data the app already stores.
  4. Quick Log stays FLOATING and unchanged — it just floats clear of the hero title. Same floating pill, same size, same accent outline, same tap target, same always-on-top behaviour over the scrolling content. Only its resting offset moves. Today it clips "LOWER BODY STRENGTH" to "LOWER BODY STRE▮", which is a real bug (register #16 recurring on Home after being marked fixed), not a matter of taste. Nothing about what Quick Log does changes.
What this deliberately does NOT do. It doesn't reorder anything, doesn't remove the Missed or Connect Health banners, and doesn't promote today's workout above the coach card — even though "what am I doing today?" being absent above the fold is arguably the biggest issue here. All of those are reorder decisions, and the June redesign rejected a Home reorder (v27–v34). If you want that reconsidered, it's a separate conversation and a separate mockup — worth having, but not something to smuggle in under "make it less wordy".

The chart's metric is a proposal, not a fixed choice — the 7-bar week could be volume, minutes, sessions or calories, and the ring could be streak, weekly-goal completion or readiness. The point is that something with a shape sits above the fold; which metric it plots is yours to pick.
